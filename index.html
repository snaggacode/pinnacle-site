About us

An Australian company based in Perth.

Pinnacle Enterprises Group Pty Ltd is a privately held Australian company, registered in Western Australia in 2026 and based in Perth. We design, develop and operate our own online businesses, holding each venture under one company so they can share the same standards, infrastructure and long-term thinking.

Our focus is on building products people genuinely want to use — software and consumer brands that are well made, honestly marketed, and built to last rather than to flip. Everything we run, we own and operate ourselves, from the first line of code to day-to-day support.

Planr.

A fitness coaching app for the App Store.

Planr brings coaches and their clients together in one place, and gives solo athletes the tools to train on their own. Coaches build and assign training programs, review their clients' progress, and stay in touch — while athletes follow their plan, log every session, and track how they're improving over time. Planr is designed, developed and published by Pinnacle Enterprises Group.

  • Build and assign structured training programs
  • Log sessions, sets, reps and effort as you train
  • Track progress, bodyweight and nutrition over time
  • Coach and client messaging to stay in sync
  • Tools for both coached athletes and solo users
